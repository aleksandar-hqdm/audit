AI visibility: listed, or just cited?

This is the question every founder asks about AI search and almost no one answers precisely: when Mentalyc shows up in an AI answer, is it being recommended as a tool to use (named in the text), or is its content just being used as a source (a mentalyc.com citation)? Across 80 questions on Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini, the answer is clear and it explains everything.

The crucial difference: cited vs recommended, by funnel stage

The pattern is stark. Your content feeds the AI answers at every stage (it is the #1 most-cited domain in the whole category, below). But that citation only turns into a recommendation at the bottom of the funnel. On informational and evaluation questions, the AI quotes you as a source and then recommends someone else.

Who is winning This is why you are winning where you win. At BOFU (buyer questions) you are both cited and recommended. At TOFU and MOFU you are cited heavily but recommended zero times: the model uses your article to answer the question, then points the reader to a competitor it found in a roundup. You are the textbook, not the product on the shelf.

Where the recommendation breaks: by engine

And even at the bottom of the funnel, the recommendation is uneven. Perplexity recommends you; ChatGPT rarely does; Gemini never does, which matters most because Gemini is Google.

Who feeds the AI answers (the citation ecosystem)

This is where to act. These are the domains the four AI engines cite most when answering category questions. Mentalyc is #1, so your content is already the reference. But look past the competitors: EHR and review sites (DeepCura, TheraPlatform, ICANotes, Headway, Ritten, Lindy, Dana Elisa) are cited heavily, and those are exactly the pages that tell the AI which tool to recommend. Get featured and ranked well inside them and you convert citation into recommendation.
