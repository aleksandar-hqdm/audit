The decline, and who absorbed it

Who is winning Twofold (+129%) and Blueprint (+27%) absorbed the demand while Mentalyc fell. Supanote (+35%) quietly passed Mentalyc. The category did not shrink. It moved.

Three root causes (not one)

1. AI Overviews took the clicks

Rankings fell ~30% but clicks fell ~74%. An AI Overview now appears on 92% of category questions (46/50), so the answer is given in-page and the visit never happens. This is a clicks problem, not a penalty.

2. Competitors out-published you

Twofold and Blueprint built huge ICD-10 / diagnosis-code libraries (anxiety ICD-10 at 14,800/mo, general-medical codes at 8k-15k each) that Mentalyc barely ranks for (present for just 1 of 10 high-volume codes tested, its lone F43.23 post), and added the buy-intent pages you are weak on.

3. Missing silos, small brand, and a conversion drop you reported

The blog (493 pages) is exposed to AI Overviews, the comparison and code silos that win the category are missing, and you are left out of most third-party roundups buyers read (absent from 5 of 6 verified). Brand search is small (~2,900/mo). You report registration conversion has fallen; the gaps above are the likely causes. (We cannot measure the rate from public tools, so that is your number, not ours.)

The Diagnosis page grades your four hypotheses against the data (including "clinicians use LLMs instead of blogs", confirmed) and diagnoses why conversion fell.

Content portfolio vs where buyers are

The imbalance in one picture

The site is heavily top-of-funnel (493 blog), the exact content AI Overviews now answer. The middle exists (122 worksheets and examples, email-gated for leads), but the bottom is thin: ~12 commercial pages and no comparison, exact-match BOFU, or populated code silo, the page types that win buyers. Recovery is about building the bottom and reclaiming the code cluster, not adding a middle.
