1. Site structure: where the silos are and are not

A full internal-link crawl (not just the sitemap) shows Mentalyc already runs the hub-and-spoke model on some sections: a /worksheets-and-cheatsheets hub (103 pages), /note-examples (19), and /help-articles (18), plus an ROI calculator. So this is not a capability gap. The fix is to extend the model you already run, not build from zero.
