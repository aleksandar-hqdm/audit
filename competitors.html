Competitors, reverse-engineered

Mentalyc does not face one competitor; it faces a different one on every front. Four direct AI-scribe rivals are out-publishing it on search, an EHR incumbent (SimplePractice) is winning the AI answers, and general-medical scribes (Heidi, Freed) own the community and the roundups. This page profiles each, with everything we verified: traffic, content silos, on-page and E-E-A-T, links, and which battleground each one actually wins.
