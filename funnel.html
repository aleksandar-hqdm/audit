Funnel: top, middle, bottom

The recovery in one line: Mentalyc built a world-class top of funnel, then AI commoditized it. Build the bottom (comparison, exact-match BOFU, and code silos), un-gate the middle so it ranks, reclaim the mental-health code cluster, turn the top into tools that capture, and stop measuring success by raw TOFU traffic.

Still heavily top-of-funnel, but the funnel is more complete than it looks: 493 blog (TOFU), 122 worksheets and examples (MOFU), product pages and an ROI calculator. The worksheets are even email-gated, so they do capture leads. The real gaps are at the bottom (thin BOFU, no comparison or code silo) and a tradeoff in the middle: the gating leaves thin on-page content, so the worksheets under-rank (you sit at #9 for "emdr note template" while Supanote, which shows the full template free, is #1).

The middle captures leads, but under-ranks

Mentalyc has the broad top (493 blog) and a real mid layer (122 worksheets and examples), and the worksheets are email-gated, so they capture leads. The tradeoff: the gated pages show thin content (~300-680 words) vs competitors' full free templates (1,600+), so they rank lower. The real gap is the bottom, thin BOFU with no comparison or code silo, not the middle.
