Diagnosis: what is actually wrong

The headline number (organic traffic down ~74%) hides the mechanism. Rankings fell meaningfully (about 30%), but clicks fell more than twice as fast (~74%). That gap is the AI-Overview effect: it is mainly a clicks problem (AI answers in-page) compounded by competitors out-publishing you, not a penalty. Below: your four hypotheses graded against the data, what you did not flag, and why conversion fell.

The cause: AI answers everywhere, and you are missing from most of them

Across the 50-question sweep, an AI Overview appears almost everywhere (90% of top-funnel, 90% of mid-funnel, 95% of buy-intent questions). So the recovery is not about finding queries with less AI: there are none. It is about being the entity the AI answer cites and recommends. Today Mentalyc is present in the AI answer for 80% of buy-intent questions but only 10% of top-funnel and 0% of mid-funnel ones.

Mentalyc still ranks #1 to #4 on most reference terms. It no longer matters when Google answers the question itself, above the result. The strategic shift is toward buy-intent and AI-citation, where intent and conversion are highest and where you already have a foothold, not because those queries escape AI (they do not).

Why registration conversion fell

We do not have your funnel analytics, so this is a reasoned set of mechanisms to confirm against your GA4, not a measured finding. The first question to settle: did the conversion rate fall, or did absolute registrations fall simply because traffic fell? The likely answer is both, for these reasons:
