CRO & metadata

The fastest revenue lever, because it lifts conversion on the traffic you already have. The messaging and trust signals are strong (demo video, 4.6 rating, 30k+ users, SOC 2). The fixable gaps are pricing prominence, proof of output (a sample note nobody shows), no comparison pages, and missing Product schema. Metadata and schema then protect presence in a zero-click SERP.

Metadata + SERP

  • Rewrite buy-intent titles/metas to match intent and add proof ('AI Note Taker for Therapists, HIPAA-compliant, 30,000+ clinicians').
  • Win the AI-Overview citation: put a clean, extractable answer block (definition, labeled example, short table) near the top of reference pages so Google pulls Mentalyc as the source even on zero-click SERPs.
  • Schema: SoftwareApplication + Offer + rating on commercial pages; FAQPage everywhere (currently inconsistent); HowTo on tool pages.
  • Featured snippets: target the #2-#8 buyer terms with concise definition-plus-list formatting.

Content funnel optimization

You already email-gate the worksheets (leads are captured), so this is about extending and tuning that, not starting it.

  • Un-gate the worksheets for SEO: the gated pages show thin content and under-rank (#9 for 'emdr note template'); show the full content free and indexable, and capture email with a soft, optional opt-in instead of a hard wall (competitors who show templates free out-rank you).
  • Extend lead capture to the blog clusters (which currently capture nothing): fillable DAP/SOAP/BIRP templates, ICD-10 quick-reference pack, treatment-plan starter, a documentation-time calculator.
  • Email nurture that moves template-downloaders to a trial with proof and sample output.
  • Intent-matched CTAs: a DAP-notes reader sees 'Generate a DAP note in 2 minutes', not a generic 'Try for free'.
