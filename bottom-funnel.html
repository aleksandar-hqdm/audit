Bottom of funnel, from a different angle

The usual BOFU view is "rank for the buyer keywords." Here that view misleads: the decision-stage searches are tiny in volume, and Mentalyc already ranks #1 on most of the brand and comparison ones. So the recovery is not about chasing buyer-search volume. The real battleground is the AI assistants, the community, and the third-party articles where the decision actually gets made, and that is where the gaps are.

Low volume does not mean low value: These decision searches barely register in keyword tools (and niche B2B volumes in those tools are unreliable to begin with), but they are the highest-intent searches in the whole funnel. Someone typing 'mentalyc vs upheal' is ready to buy. A handful of them a month are quality leads, so 'no volume' is not the same as 'no leads', and the fact that you already rank #1 here is a sign of strength to build on, not ignore.

1. The decision-search layer is thin, you lead it, so build on it

The brand-conquest and comparison searches everyone assumes are a battleground have almost no volume, and Mentalyc ranks #1 on most of the ones with any. That is a position of strength on a shoestring. The move is not to ignore it; it is to invest a little and own it: turn those one-off #1 rankings into a real /compare silo with strong, high-converting comparison and 'alternatives' pages, capture the high-intent visitors who do arrive, and harvest the links and AI-citations those pages generate (Twofold's /compare earned 170 referring domains).

For precision: the genuine category-buyer cluster on the Keyword Universe page is only ~2,500/mo; the brand-conquest and comparison terms specifically ('vs', 'review', 'alternatives') are ~0 in the tools. Low recorded volume, high intent.

2. What you are actually missing: the off-site decision sources

The buyer decision is made in places that are not your site: independent roundup articles, community threads, and AI answers. A competitor does not need to beat your pages; it just needs to be the pick in those sources.

Third-party 'best AI scribe' articles, re-verified in a live browser

I rendered each roundup that ranks for 'best AI scribe / note-taker for therapists' (full JavaScript, not raw HTML) and checked every brand. Mentalyc is absent from 5 of the 6 verified, including all the independent ones. It appears in only one (Freed's own list), at #4. And the author always ranks itself first (Freed lists Freed #1, Berries lists Berries #1).

Community (Reddit): a relative strength, with real gaps

Here Mentalyc does well. Across 14 'best AI scribe' threads it is the #2 most-mentioned brand (18 mentions, just behind Heidi's 20, ahead of Freed's 17). But it is completely absent from 6 of the 14 threads, including several of the most active ones where Heidi and Freed dominate. So community is a strength to defend and extend, not a gap to fix from scratch.

Reddit, by the numbers

  • In the threads it appears in, it is often the top or near-top mention

The play: seed and earn authentic mentions in the threads you are missing from (r/therapists, r/psychotherapy).
