The honest headline: No single channel replaces the ~170k lost monthly visits, and it should not try, because that traffic barely converted. The portfolio recovers registrations and revenue at lower traffic by capturing higher-intent demand and converting more of what arrives.

Measurement plan

Leading (weekly): visit-to-trial by landing page, email captures per cluster, BOFU ranks, AI-Overview citation incidence for mentalyc.com, share of recommendation across the four assistants, referring-domain quality, branded search.

Lagging (monthly): registrations and trials by source, trial-to-paid, new MRR by source, organic sessions to commercial and tool pages.

Reframed north star: Registrations from non-TOFU sources plus brand and assisted demand. Reporting recovery as raw organic visits will both understate progress and mislead strategy.
