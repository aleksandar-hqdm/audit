The gap PR has to close: awareness, not authority

Brand demand is soft. Searches for 'mentalyc' run about 2,900 a month, down from a ~3,600 peak a year ago, and all branded searches together (about 4,000 a month) are roughly 0.3% of the ~1.48M searches in the category. The links and the domain authority are fine; what is thin is how many people know the brand and how many third-party places vouch for it. That is a PR problem, and PR is where a well-funded position pays off.

Asset 1: your user database (original-data PR)

Aggregate, anonymized, and publish what only Mentalyc can see. Original data earns links from journalists, associations, and academics, and becomes the thing AI assistants cite when asked about the category.

Asset 2: customer feedback (niche, story-led PR)

Turn the testimonials and reviews into earned media aimed squarely at the therapist niche, not generic tech press.

  • Therapist outcome stories: 'how I got my evenings back', private-practice owners scaling without burnout. Pitch to counseling and social-work trade media and podcasts.
  • Association partnerships: co-marketing and member webinars with NASW, AAMFT, ACA, state boards, and training providers like PESI (where competitors already rank).
  • Therapist-creator program: seed honest reviews from credible clinician creators on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube; this both earns links and plants the community proof the AI assistants and Reddit-led SERPs cite.
  • Conference presence into earned media: you already know clinicians do not recognize the brand at conferences. Convert talks and booths into press, recorded sessions, and recognizable faces.

Reverse-engineered: the sites the AI assistants actually cite

When ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google's AI Overview recommend an AI scribe, they synthesize from a specific set of third-party pages. We logged every source cited across the question sweep. Mentalyc.com is already the single most-cited domain (37 citations, ahead of every competitor), but the sites that carry the recommendation, the EHR directories and the 'best AI scribe' roundups, mostly do not feature it. Getting placed and ranked inside these is the most direct path to becoming the named pick, not just the cited source.
