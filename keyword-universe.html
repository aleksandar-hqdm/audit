Where the demand actually is: the topical clusters

The category is not one giant top-of-funnel blob; it is a set of topical clusters. Here it is broken out and deduped (each concept counted once, not once per phrasing), so you can see the real shape: the two diagnosis-code clusters dominate the volume, and they are exactly where Mentalyc is thinnest. The clusters Mentalyc already covers (templates, examples, assessments) are the smaller ones.

The strategic tension

The category is dominated by clinical-reference clusters, not buyer terms. The two biggest are diagnosis codes: a ~133k/mo mental-health cluster (your on-audience target, where you rank on just 11% of concepts and Blueprint wins) and a separate ~286k general-medical cluster (hypokalemia, heart failure) that Twofold owns as a general medical scribe (off-audience unless you expand beyond therapy). The clusters you already cover (templates 50%, examples 45%, assessments 51%) are the smaller ones. And the genuine buy-intent core is tiny (~2,500/mo). So you cannot recover on buyer keywords alone: win the mental-health code cluster back as tools, and convert far better.

Reading the colours: grey = off-audience or fragmented long-tail · orange = on-audience, Mentalyc is mostly absent (build here) · teal = on-audience, Mentalyc already covers.
