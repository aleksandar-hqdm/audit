Backlinks & authority

The contrarian finding, and it holds up under the data: links are not Mentalyc's problem. The profile has the most referring domains in the set, it is still growing, and it carries a cleaner spam profile than the fastest-growing competitor. So generic link-building is the wrong place to spend. Three link actions do matter: clean up a real toxic-link signature, point new links at the revenue pages, and earn placements on the sites the AI assistants cite.

Who is winning Mentalyc has the most referring domains of the set (1,252) and a cleaner spam profile (13) than Twofold (31), which grew +129% partly on bought links. On raw authority rank Mentalyc (252) sits ahead of Twofold (181) and behind Blueprint (276) and Upheal (312), so it is competitive, not the bottleneck. Growth in this category is content-driven, not link-driven.

Your link profile is growing, not decaying

If links were the cause of the traffic loss, you would see them falling. They are not. Mentalyc added referring domains every month over the past year, from 674 to 1,191, while keeping lost domains low. The link graph is healthy; the traffic loss is a content and AI-Overview story, not a link story.

The one real link problem: a toxic-link signature to disavow

Three link-vendor anchors stand out in the profile, and they are unmistakable: Telegram and PBN sellers pointing spammy domains at the site. Together they account for about 129 referring domains. These are exactly the kind of links a spam update penalizes, so they are the first thing to clean up.

Do not chase Twofold's links: most of them are spam

It is tempting to copy the links of the competitor that grew +129%. Do not. Of 177 domains that link to Twofold but not to Mentalyc, only three are clean and relevant; the rest are largely PBN and expired-domain spam (about one in five scored 50 or higher on spam, and most carry no authority at all). Twofold grew on content and breadth, with a spammy link tail you do not want to imitate.

The links that move the needle now: the sites AI assistants cite

Because raw authority is already competitive, the high-value targets are not generic. They are the specific directories, EHR review sites and roundups the AI assistants pull from when they recommend a tool. A placement or a strong ranking on those swings the recommendation, not just the link count.
